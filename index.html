From a young age most of my free time was spent on them, and as the years went on that passion evolved. From playing, to understanding how they worked, to wanting to build my own. A friend showed me raw code and I immediately knew that's what I wanted to do. I quit my electrical apprenticeship, went to college, then Northumbria to pursue it.

My skillset doesn't stop at games. The low-level thinking, system design, and code quality standards that game development demands are equally at home in a software engineering context.

Projects

Factor-5

A 2D arcade space shooter built in C++ inspired by Galaga. Features escalating enemy behaviour across difficulty tiers, a missile targeting system, power-ups, unique bullet patterns, and an event-driven architecture handling collision, targeting, and game state.

Hornet Engine C++ 2D Prototype Project

Enemy behaviour scales with the level. They start stationary, then introducing lateral movement, increasing velocity, new bullet patterns, and eventually fully free-roaming with randomised speed and locations.

Pointer usage was central to the targeting systems enemy and player alike. Missiles track enemies by referencing object coordinates directly, keeping targeting responsive without unnecessary overhead. The biggest technical challenge was getting the missile system to fire cleanly under load without lag or sound spam/reapting rapidly, which required careful management of pointer and event usage.

Built against a deadline as a university assessment, demonstrating clean pointer usage, object-oriented design, and playable game feel within a constrained scope. It scored 92% and was praised for its polish and technical implementation.

First Blood

A 3D third-person horror mini game built in Unreal Engine 5, designed as a standalone experience within a shared multiplayer meta game. Collect data points against the clock while avoiding a proximity-triggered monster.

Game Development Unreal Engine C++ 3D Prototype Project

First Blood is a 3D horror mini game within a meta game that seamlessly integrates with the larger experience it needed to be implemented in. The player must collect data points scattered across a dimly lit map whilst avoiding a proximity-triggered AI monster and navigating an interactable map within the time limit.

The boss AI uses a proximity trigger. It is dormant until the player enters its range, then actively chases and deals damage. HP and stamina are tracked as real values with on-screen bars, feeding into the tension of deciding when to run and when to push for another data point.

Everything was built from the ground up to the map layout, AI behaviour, UI and game logic. Asset packs were used for models and textures however. A hidden secret mode rewards players who complete the run with a higher score, unlocking a red-tinted super success state for bonus points.

The primary focus was on atmosphere, AI responsiveness, and making the time pressure feel genuinely stressful rather than arbitrary.

Soul Chamber

A Roblox horror survival prototype built in Luau. Players evade roaming monsters across a custom built map, with sprint, flashlight and health mechanics, a points system, and a full menu screen.

Game Development Roblox Engine Luau 3D Demo

Built as a solo prototype with basic multiplayer, this project was a test of my ability to create a horror survival experience within Roblox Studio and its Luau language.

I analysed existing Roblox horror games to understand what made them effective, then built a custom map with a focus on lighting, sound design, and player movement with respected mechanics e.g. sprinting. Animation work was also explored and altered to further enhance the player and monster's design and movements.

I took an existing AI implementation, analysed how it worked, modified its behaviour and integrated it into my own gameplay systems to create the monsters that chase the player to fit the game's pacing and horror tone whilst tuning chase logic and responsiveness.

A full menu screen was also added with a points system to create a sense of progression that rewarded players for long survival times, this tested my ability to manage player data. Multiplayer was intended, however due to Roblox's ID requriements I could not go further
