The Project Brief

Used by the client today at Just In Tyne Escape, this project is a full escape room experience built by a team of 6 that I managed and led as the project manager, lead programmer and director. I was responsible for the project plan, team management, technical implementation, task delegation and general direction of the project.

Constructed as a University Project, this escape room was designed around the client's needs and specifications - an escape room with a suitable theme for a family audience, an engaging narrative with a twist and two phases that would last a total of eleven weeks. Phase one would consist of the players going to smaller businesses around the local area to collect clues and information within ten weeks. Those clues would then be used in phase two to solve the final puzzle using a custom made program to unlock one of three possible endings.


Production Process

Meetings

To deepen the understanding of the project and the client's needs, my deputy manager and I organised two in-person meetings at the Just In Tyne Escape office with the client to discuss the project in more detail. This included a two hour session discussing scope, requirements, the narrative, restrictions, ideas, and overall vision. After these meetings concluded, I organised a team meeting to discuss the project to inform the team of the client's needs and how we would achieve them.


Task Delegation

Once the team was up to speed, I created a project plan and timeline in excel to delegate tasks to the team and track overall progress. Furthermore, it ensured that the project had structure and would be completed ahead of schedule. Tasks were delegated to team members based on their strengths and weaknesses, as well as their availability. The tasks themselves were split up into smaller, manageable objectives to avoid overwhelming the team. To further mitigate this, team members were paired up to lessen the overall workload.

Additionally it also allowed team members to raise any issues they had about their tasks and request assistance from other team members or me. This was a key part of the project as it allowed me to ensure that team members could complete their tasks effectively and efficiently.


Personal Involvement

Outside of project management, I delegated difficult tasks to myself. This includes: immersive videos, general character design, general programming, police report files, and final delivery to the client. Moreover, I was responsible for direct communication with the client to ensure that the project was not only to their specifications and liking, but to keep them informed about the progress and possible delivery date.


Custom Terminal Program

A key part of the development process was constructing a custom terminal program that would be used by players to interact with to solve puzzles and pieces of the story. The program was written in C++ and was designed to handle player input, error handling and provide responsive feedback to the player whilst also being immersive.

To do that, I split up the group into sections to work on different parts of the program. The first team laid down the code foundation based on the pseudocode code I provide. The second team which I was part of polished and advanced the code further to make it a fully functional program. And the final team worked on final polish, design and testing to ensure that the program was fully functional and immersive.


Final Product

Phase one and two of the project were completed. The team responded well to the project plan and the following were made:


  • Immersive Videos - Used to set the scene and provide context for the player.
  • Ten Created Characters and Case Files - Each character has a unique backstory and the case files that make them suspects.
  • Clues and Evidence - Used to provide players with information to solve the mystery.
  • Interactive Puzzles - Core gameplay loop for the escape room used to challenge the player's problem solving skills.
  • Custom Terminal Program (C++) - The main interface for interacting with the puzzle system in phase two.
  • Immersive Ending Video - Provides a satisfying conclusion to the player's experience.

The entirety of our plans for the project were complete and the client was very satisfied with the results.
