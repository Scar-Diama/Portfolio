Overview

I am the primary creator and maintainer of high-impact fan modifications for Dragon Ball Z: Kakarot, operating under the handle TheReckon5. My work focuses on expanding the game’s transformation systems, skill sets, and gameplay options through carefully designed, freely distributed content for all to enjoy who have the game. My profile has over 220,000 unique downloads and my flag ship mod "Limitless" has been featured on multiple gaming news outlets and has been viewed by millions of people worldwide. My work has been praised for its quality, creativity, and attention to detail, and I have received positive feedback from the community for my contributions.


Technical Approach

I utilised UAssetGUI to edit Unreal Engine .uasset files and Asset Editor to package the modified assets. This involved extracting relevant files, modifying internal data structures to create new entries, establishing the necessary linkages to existing game systems, and integrating the resulting content. New skills and transformation forms were developed, frequently by combining and adapting elements from existing assets to produce cohesive, functional additions that expanded player options beyond the base game.


Flagship Mod - Limitless

My primary release, Limitless, is a comprehensive expansion pack that adds numerous custom transformations, skills, combo strings, and supporting systems for multiple playable characters (including variants of Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego, Beast, Super Saiyan 4, and related forms).

Limitless has received extensive coverage from YouTube creators, including channels such as MGGamez, RikudouFox, and SLOPlays. Collectively, these videos have accumulated millions of views, contributing significantly to the mod’s visibility. Click here to view a selected video covering a Limitless Update I released.


Final Product

Phase one and two of the project were completed. The team responded well to the project plan and the following were made:


  • Immersive Videos - Used to set the scene and provide context for the player.
  • Ten Created Characters and Case Files - Each character has a unique backstory and the case files that make them suspects.
  • Clues and Evidence - Used to provide players with information to solve the mystery.
  • Interactive Puzzles - Core gameplay loop for the escape room used to challenge the player's problem solving skills.
  • Custom Terminal Program (C++) - The main interface for interacting with the puzzle system in phase two.
  • Immersive Ending Video - Provides a satisfying conclusion to the player's experience.

The entirety of our plans for the project were complete and the client was very satisfied with the results.
