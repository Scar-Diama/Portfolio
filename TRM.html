Overview

I am the primary creator and maintainer of high-impact fan modifications for Dragon Ball Z: Kakarot, operating under the handle TheReckon5 . My work focuses on expanding the game’s transformation systems, skill sets, and gameplay options through carefully designed, freely distributed content for all who have the game on PC in their Steam library. My profile has over 220,000 unique downloads and my flagship mod "Limitless" has been featured on multiple gaming channels and has been collectively viewed by millions of people worldwide. My work has been praised for its quality, creativity, and attention to detail, and I have received positive feedback from the community for my contributions.


Technical Approach

I utilised UAssetGUI to edit Unreal Engine uasset files and Kai Heilos' Asset Editor to package the modified assets (both of these tools are free-to-use public modding tools). This involved extracting relevant files, modifying internal data structures to create new entries, establishing the necessary linkages to existing game systems, and integrating the resulting content. Such content included: new skills, transformations, features, buffs and progression beyond the base game.

Additionally, I put together a working team that allowed me to split the workload to prioritise the programming sections from the artistic sections. The team members were given tasks such as new icon creations to draw for new transformations.


Flagship Mod - Limitless

My primary release, Limitless, is a comprehensive expansion pack that adds numerous custom transformations, skills, combo strings, and supporting systems for multiple playable characters (including variants of Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego, Beast, Super Saiyan 4, and related forms). The mod is also compatible with other mods such as 'VSMode,' which required mod author cooperation to achieve.

Limitless has received extensive coverage from YouTube creators, including channels such as theMGamez, RikudouFox, and SLOplays.
